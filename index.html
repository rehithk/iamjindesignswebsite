Our Process

We follow a thoughtful, personalized design journey to bring your dream space to life.

Understanding You

We begin by getting to know you, your needs, preferences, lifestyle, and vision for the space. We also study your site, floor plans, budget, and timeline to build the right foundation.

Planning the Layout

Based on our discussions, we create floor plans, furniture layouts, and structural suggestions. We also share a clear design scope and fee for approval.

Design Concept

We present mood boards, design ideas, and reference images to help you visualize the space. Your feedback helps us refine the concept and estimate the budget.

Design Details

After finalizing the concept, we create technical drawings and a detailed material list covering furniture, electricals, plumbing, and more.

Execution & Supervision

Our team supervises the site to ensure timely progress, quality work, and smooth coordination. You stay updated every step of the way.

Final Touch & Handover

We add the final styling, inspect every detail, and hand over a beautiful, finished space, just as you imagined, or even better.

About Us

I AM JIN DESIGNS

Where every space tells your story.

At I Am Jin Designs, we open the door to a world where creativity meets comfort. Your space is reimagined with style, soul, and substance.

Founded and run by a passionate designer couple, our studio is built on a shared love for interiors and a deep belief in the power of thoughtful design. Every project we take on is personal, it's not just about how it looks, but how it feels.

Rooted in our philosophy, "Line to Luxury," we approach each space as a journey from the first sketch to the final touch. Whether you're working with a modest budget or dreaming of luxury, we give every detail the same level of dedication, creativity, and care.

From contemporary elegance to timeless classics, from bold statements to minimalist calm, we design to reflect you. Trends inspire us, but your lifestyle leads the way.

We don't just decorate rooms, we craft meaningful spaces with warmth, character, and a timeless charm you'll love coming home to.
